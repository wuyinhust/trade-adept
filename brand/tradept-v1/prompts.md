# tradept logo prompts

工具：内置 image_gen，透明背景。
模板：gpt-image-2-style-library / Brand Identity Package（品牌身份包），按单个 Logo 资产调整。

## 横版字标

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Design a finished, original website logo for the brand "tradept", an external-trade order and team workspace SaaS. This is a single final logo asset, not a brand board, not multiple concepts.

Brand character: approachable, confident, optimistic, organized, useful. Take abstract inspiration from the friendly lowercase typography and fresh color confidence of monday.com, and the clear blue enterprise trust of Salesforce. Invent a distinct symbol and typography. Do not reproduce either brand's capsules or cloud silhouette.

Composition: one horizontal lockup, compact standalone abstract icon on the left, lowercase wordmark "tradept" on the right. Exact lettering: t r a d e p t, all lowercase, no space, no punctuation, no other text. Generous but not excessive gap between the icon and wordmark. Icon about the same optical height as the full wordmark including p descender. Logo must occupy most of a wide image, with only modest 6 percent clear space at the edges. Aspect ratio approximately 3.5:1.

Symbol: a distinctive bold geometric lowercase "t" inspired by two connecting, flowing trade routes; two thick clean rounded ribbons meet to form a simple recognizable T-like symbol, with a subtle forward-motion feel. Cobalt-blue top crossbar rounds smoothly down into a blue vertical stem; a short teal outgoing curve completes the lower right. The outline must be simple enough to redraw precisely as 2-3 vector paths and recognizable at 24px. Keep the symbol coherent; avoid generic sync arrows, infinity symbol, network nodes, globes, containers or ships.

Typography: custom soft geometric sans-serif, semibold to bold, round terminals and generous open counters; professional and compact like a high-quality SaaS wordmark; slightly tighter optical kerning. Both letters t share the same distinctive friendly shape. Entire wordmark solid deep ink navy #172B4D, not separate multicolored letters.

Palette: icon uses solid bright cobalt #2563EB and fresh teal #18BFA6. Wordmark deep ink #172B4D. Solid flat colors. No gradients, shadows, texture, photorealism, bevels, 3D, decorations, watermark or mockup. Pixel-crisp, optically balanced, polished vector-style geometry.

Output: real transparent background with alpha; only the full horizontal logo. No white canvas, no checkerboard drawn into the pixels. This must work as a reusable navigation-bar brand asset on a light website.
```

## 独立图标

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Edit the referenced tradept logo into its standalone icon asset. Preserve EXACTLY the design of the icon at the left of the reference: same silhouette, same proportional geometry, same cobalt blue lowercase t with the same teal outgoing lower-right curve. Remove the entire wordmark "tradept". Do not redesign the symbol. Render the symbol centered on a square canvas, filling 78 percent of the width and height, with transparent padding on all sides. Refine the icon into perfectly flat solid color fills, blue #2563EB and teal #18BFA6, clean crisp edges, absolutely no gradients, shadows or texture. No text, no other elements. Output real transparent background with alpha.
```
